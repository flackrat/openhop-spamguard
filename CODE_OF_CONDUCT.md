# Code of conduct

SpamGuard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people whatever their background or experience,
and keep criticism about the work, never the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project meets.

It also matters here because SpamGuard sits on a shared radio network: never post anything from real mesh
traffic, such as names, messages, places or callsigns, in an issue or pull request. Make up any example you
need (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, tell the maintainer privately through GitHub's private reporting form:
<https://github.com/flackrat/openhop-spamguard/security/advisories/new>. Start the title with "Conduct" so
it's clear it isn't a security report. You can also use GitHub's own **Report content** option on any
comment. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer will acknowledge a report within 7 days, look at what happened, and may ask the person to
change their behaviour, remove their comments, or block them from the project, depending on how serious it
was. The person who reported it will be told the outcome, without details that would identify anyone else.
